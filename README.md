# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive and comprehensive **Power BI / Excel Sales Dashboard** created to analyze the performance and key business metrics of **E-Commerce**. This project visualizes sales trends, profit margins, top-performing product categories, payment channel preferences, and regional performance across various states.

---

## 🛠️ Key Features & Insights

* **KPI Tracking:** Real-time visibility into major metrics:
  * **Sum of Amount:** Total Revenue generated ($438K)
  * **Sum of Profit:** Total Profit earned ($37K)
  * **Sum of Quantity:** Total units sold (6K)
  * **Average Order Value (AOV):** Average spent per order ($120.90K)

* **Monthly Profit Analysis:** Month-over-month profit visualization identifying peak sales periods throughout the year.
* **State-wise Sales Distribution:** Regional revenue breakdown highlighting top markets like Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Gujarat, and others.
* **Customer Insights:** Identification of top customers based on order value and quantity.
* **Category & Sub-Category Performance:** 
  * Breakdown of total quantity sold by Category (**Clothing: 62.62%**, **Electronics: 20.55%**, **Furniture: 16.83%**).
  * Profit distribution across sub-categories including Printers, Bookcases, Sarees, Accessories, and Tables.
* **Payment Mode Breakdown:** Analysis of customer payment choices (**COD**, **UPI**, **Credit Card**, **Debit Card**, **EMI**).

## 🚀 How to View & Use

1. **View PDF Report:** Open `Sales Dashboard.pdf` directly to explore the static layout and visual insights.
2. **Interactive File:** Open the dashboard file (`.pbix` / `.xlsx`) in Power BI Desktop or Microsoft Excel to interact with dynamic filters, slicers, and cross-highlighting features.

## 📊 Summary of Recommendations

1. **Inventory Focus:** Capitalize on **Clothing**, as it contributes to over 62% of product quantity sold.
2. **Regional Expansion:** Scale marketing efforts in high-revenue states like Delhi, Gujarat, and Bihar.
3. **Payment Options:** Promote digital payments (UPI & Cards) while maintaining Cash on Delivery (COD) to support majority preferences.
